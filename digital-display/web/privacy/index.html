Privacy Policy

Digital Display (display.datpl.com) by Digital Arts · Last updated 15 November 2026

What Digital Display is

Digital Display lets businesses show their photos and videos on TVs. You manage your TVs and playlists at display.datpl.com; the Digital Display TV app plays them.

Information we keep

Your account e-mail address and business name; your playlists and settings; the names, links and IDs of the cloud folders and files you add; and for each linked TV its name, screen size, app version and when it was last online. Older accounts may also have files they uploaded to us before November 2026.

Your files stay in your own cloud storage

Digital Display does not copy or store your photos and videos on our servers. When you connect Google Drive, you choose one folder. To let your TVs play it, Digital Display sets that folder to "Anyone with the link can view" (we tell you before doing this). Your TVs then download the files directly from Google and keep a copy on the TV so they keep playing offline.

Google user data

When you click "Connect Google Drive", Google asks for permission for the drive.file scope only. This lets Digital Display see and use only the folder or files you pick in Google's own picker — not the rest of your Drive. We use this permission only to (1) show your chosen folder and its photos and videos in your dashboard and (2) change that folder's sharing to "anyone with the link can view" so your TVs can play it. The Google sign-in token stays in your browser for that moment and is not stored on our servers. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can remove a folder in the dashboard at any time, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To stop sharing the folder, change its sharing back to "Restricted" in Google Drive.

Free plan

On the Free plan, short Digital Arts / Digital Display ads are shown on your TVs between your own items. We do not track who watches your TV.

Where data is kept

Account data is stored with our hosting provider Supabase (servers in Mumbai, India). We keep it while your account is active. To delete your account and its data, contact us.
